# Policy dataset and assessment methodology

Source: user-provided `Data.pdf`, 18 pages, received 26 October 2026. These figures are transcribed from a third-party/user document and **not independently verified** against current insurer contracts. Accordingly no “unconfirmed”/“needs verification” status is used for the six source-described categories; however the app retains a short source caveat.

## Need model
Need level 0 Low, 1 Optimal, 2 Required, 3 Strongly required. Hospital/surgery need increases with age band (70+); specialist and outpatient need increases with more frequent visits, chronic needs and specialist use; medication need increases with chronic disease and medicine usage. Emergency coverage baseline is Required, Strongly required for older cohort.

## Benefit model
Benefit breadth levels 0 not established, 1 narrow, 2 conditional, 3 substantial, 4 broad. Each of 16 named products has descriptions and annual limits. Emergency and routine outpatient benefits are conservatively described where the source only discusses post-admission or special treatments.

## Match
Category expected benefit levels = [1,2,3,4]. Good match if offered >= expected, Partly covered if one level behind, Needs review if two+ behind. Frequent outpatient needs with limited specialist/GP coverage are explicitly downgraded. Overall weighted score: hospital 25%, surgery 15%, specialists 15%, emergency 15%, medication 15%, outpatient 15%. Score >=85 Generally suitable, >=65 Potential gaps, else Likely mismatch. Residency not citizen/PR for MediShield Life triggers Eligibility review.

## Limitations
Not a medical/clinical or underwriting judgment. Absence of rider/actual policy version, pre-existing condition exclusion information and insurer verification requires human confirmation. Source includes indicative premium ranges, but these are not quoted as exact individual premiums, and costs screen remains user-entered until more precise age-next-birthday and subsidy inputs are captured.
