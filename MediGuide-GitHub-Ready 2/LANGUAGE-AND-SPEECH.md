# Language and speech behavior (2026-11-19)

- The header language selector offers English, Simplified Chinese and Tamil. Bundled dictionaries translate primary navigation, common page headings, onboarding labels/options, action headings and selected direct Plan better recommendations.
- The Gemini chatbot receives the selected language and is instructed to respond in it.
- To avoid distorting insurance contract meaning, plan names, medicine names, insurer names, numerical limits and source policy wording are retained in English where a reviewed translation is not available. This is not a certified translation of policy terms; a professional insurance-language review is recommended before relying on translated contractual descriptions.
- Speech recognition uses `en-SG`, `zh-CN` or `ta-IN` based on the selected language.
- Speech synthesis chooses an installed voice for the selected language, preferring voices whose names suggest enhanced/natural quality and uses slower pacing. Actual voice quality depends on installed browser/OS voices; the website cannot install a voice or guarantee natural voice availability.
- Voice recognition may be processed by the browser vendor's speech service. The transcript is shown to the user and is not sent until the user presses Send.
