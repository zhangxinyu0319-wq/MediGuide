# Personalization methodology

The user's broad health conditions and any optional medicine names are mapped to relevant clinical categories for educational display. Medicine-to-condition associations are not diagnoses, may have more than one indication and do not imply claim eligibility. For example, lisinopril is frequently used for hypertension and may have other uses.

The six-category fit score is a *heuristic*, not a medical risk score or guarantee. The comparison considers policy-level benefit breadth and user needs; insurer-specific underwriting, pre-existing conditions, coverage exclusions, riders and actual reimbursement are not known.

Most Integrated Shield Plans focus on inpatient and specified hospital-linked treatment, not ordinary ongoing prescriptions or GP visits. MediSave CDMP eligibility and drug subsidies are distinct from MediShield Life policy benefit reimbursement. Do not claim that a user’s medicine is insured merely because its condition appears in CDMP or an insurer's broad specialty list.

Sources: user-provided Data.pdf (18 pages) and More data.pdf (7 pages). Neither source was independently confirmed against official current policy documents. Where documents are ambiguous or disagree, wording is qualified.
